## Post-run diagnosis and v4.6 decision

The completed 50k development freeze selected `tau=inf`. Relative to its exact
local FixMatch tuning reference, finite tau values 2, 5, and 10 changed bACC by
-12.12, -10.37, and -9.68 percentage points; `tau=inf` changed it by +1.77.
Thus the executed v4.5 full method disabled the norm cap and was identical to
the registered PCGrad/no-cap operator. This is a component-identity failure,
not evidence that tau merely needs to be increased on CIFAR-100.

For the C100 full run, 248,067/250,000 steps had eligible geometry (99.23%),
63,547 updated the pooled anchor (25.42%), and 233,163/248,067 eligible steps
were conflicting (93.99%). Only two invalid anchor updates occurred. The
failure is therefore not primarily caused by rare eligibility, a support
threshold that prevents all updates, or an invalid anchor. The pooled anchor
also combines 33 tail classes across time, while the surgery replaces the
complete classifier gradient of accepted predicted-head examples; sampled
deep diagnostics show those examples were overwhelmingly truly head. This
operator is insufficiently tail-specific.

The v4.6 recovery changes the unit of intervention to each tail classifier
output row, maintains one supervised self-row EMA anchor per tail class, and
bounds only the conflicting correction with a per-class norm budget. It leaves
all head/medium classifier rows and the backbone gradient unchanged. Five
paired 50k C100 development runs isolate baseline, legacy global projection,
tail-row restriction, classwise anchors, and the correction budget before any
new 250k spend.
